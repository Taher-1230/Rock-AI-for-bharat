# JanSaarthi AI  
### Multilingual Government Scheme Eligibility & Simplification Platform

🌐 Live Demo: https://jansaarthi-ai.netlify.app/

---

## 🚀 Overview

JanSaarthi AI is a cloud-based, AI-powered civic access platform designed to improve awareness and accessibility of government welfare schemes for underserved communities in India.

The platform simplifies complex government policies into clear, actionable guidance and provides personalized scheme recommendations based on verified user inputs.

Built for inclusion, scalability, and responsible AI deployment.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Millions of citizens miss eligible welfare benefits due to:

- Complex legal and bureaucratic language  
- Scattered government portals  
- Lack of personalized discovery  
- Language barriers  
- Low digital literacy  

Citizens often rely on intermediaries, leading to misinformation and missed opportunities.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

JanSaarthi AI:

- Enables Aadhaar-based identity verification (consent-based)
- Filters schemes using rule-based eligibility logic
- Uses AI (Amazon Bedrock) to simplify policy language
- Provides multilingual explanations (Gujarati & Hindi)
- Offers step-by-step application guidance
- Is built on AWS for scalable deployment

---

## 🤖 Meaningful Use of AI

AI is used for:

- Converting complex government documents into simplified explanations  
- Generating contextual guidance based on user profile  
- Translating scheme details into regional languages  

Rule-based logic handles eligibility filtering.  
AI handles interpretation, simplification, and personalization.

Powered by **Amazon Bedrock**.

## 🔐 Authentication & Responsible Design
